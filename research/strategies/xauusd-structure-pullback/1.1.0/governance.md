# Strategy governance — xauusd-structure-pullback@1.1.0

Version 1.1.0 is a semantic clarification and executable preregistration created before any official backtest. It is not claimed to be performance-equivalent to version 1.0.0.

No backtest results were examined or used to choose these definitions. No parameter was optimized. The definitions favor the simplest conservative interpretation of the frozen thesis and the immutable evidence already emitted by the research detectors.

| v1.0.0 language | Ambiguity | v1.1.0 definition | Ex-ante reason and alternatives | Dependency / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direction `BOTH` | Dependent rules were not fully directional. | LONG and SHORT have separate bias, transition, sweep, FVG, zone, stop, and target sides. | Preserves the declared scope; LONG-only and SHORT-only were rejected as silent narrowing. | MSS/CHOCH, sweep, FVG, target records. |
| Liquidity sweep required | No side, sequence, age, relation, or selector. | LONG SELL_SIDE; SHORT BUY_SIDE; strictly before transition; at most 8 completed M15 candles; no intervening opposite transition; source swing appears in prior snapshot; latest then ID. | Eight bars is a short fixed setup window and was chosen before results. Same-candle and post-shift alternatives weaken causal ordering. | Immutable accepted StructuralLiquiditySweepEvent only. `consumedSource` remains eligible because it records the accepted sweep's deterministic source consumption. |
| Shift with displacement | Composer lacked exact evidence binding. | Accepted MSS/CHOCH breakout candle must match an immutable displacement observation and directional close; strict 1.1 ATR and 0.65 body/range thresholds remain unchanged. | Retains v1.0.0 numeric semantics. No new displacement threshold. | Existing displacement observation must be supplied; it is never recomputed by the composer. |
| FVG “created by or immediately following” shift | “Immediately” and candle relationship were undefined. | Transition breakout candle is FVG C2; C3 is exactly the next completed candle; direction matches; select earliest then stable ID; ACTIVE/PARTIALLY_MITIGATED only. | C2 is the displacement source candle and the next close is the minimum replay-safe confirmation. C1/C3 and wider windows were considered but add weaker association. | Immutable FVG lifecycle record. |
| Retracement zone | No boundary or remaining/original rule. | Entire original FVG, inclusive boundaries; expires 20 completed candles after creation or 4 after first touch. | Original FVG most directly preserves v1.0.0 “gap”; midpoint/halves introduce an unsupported refinement. | Immutable original bounds and lifecycle timestamps. |

| Allowed session | Boundary, weekday, holiday, and last-candle behavior incomplete. | Fixed UTC 07:00–16:00 and 13:00–22:00, start inclusive/end exclusive, UTC Monday–Friday, no holiday model; trigger close and next open both eligible. | Preserves frozen UTC windows and avoids inferring city DST semantics. | Deterministic UTC calendar math. | Minimum RR 2.0 | Equality, invalid prices, cost stage, farther search unclear. | Positive risk and reward; RR ≥ 2.0; equality qualifies; nearest failure INVALID; execution costs excluded from structural composition. | Preserves numeric threshold and avoids mixing market-structure facts with simulation costs. | Exact entry, stop, target required. |
| One entry per structure event | Identity and consumption were undefined. | Transition regime ID, falling back to event ID; consume only at fill through immutable consumption record; persists across sessions; no re-entry after stop. | Regime ID is the detector's stable setup identity. Attempts and invalid setups do not constitute entries. | Requires immutable consumption records / reducer. |
| Cancellation and exits | Lifecycle, counting, gaps, and conflicts incomplete. | Explicit pre-entry invalidations; entry candle counts as holding candle 1; exit on candle 32 close; structural-close, stop, target, max-hold precedence; adverse OHLC convention; governed gap prices. | Conservative precedence acknowledges unknowable intrabar chronology. | Current generic backtester needs a structural execution adapter. |

Known limitations: fixed UTC research windows do not model holidays; OHLC data cannot reveal intrabar chronology; execution readiness depends on the versioned structural adapter and immutable consumption records. These limitations do not authorize an official backtest.
