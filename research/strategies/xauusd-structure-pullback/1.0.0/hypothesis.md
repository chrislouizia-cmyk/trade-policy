# XAUUSD Structure Pullback 1.0.0 — Hypothesis

This immutable research candidate tests whether a completed, session-qualified market-structure shift with meaningful displacement, followed by a retracement into its originating unmitigated imbalance or structural zone, produces positive expected-cost out-of-sample expectancy when risk and reward are anchored to confirmed structure.

It is a preregistered hypothesis, not a profitability claim. It was specified without optimizing against the rejected `gold-intraday-research@1.0.0` results.

The candidate is intentionally marked `DETECTOR_IMPLEMENTATION_REQUIRED`. Rolling highs/lows, a generic breakout, or EMA relationships may not substitute for confirmed swing structure or a market-structure shift.
